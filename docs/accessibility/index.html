Accessibility statement

Information and support should be usable without unnecessary barriers

The MentorSphere aims to make its website, communication and online sessions clear, flexible and accessible to people with different sensory, cognitive, physical and communication needs.

Web statement

Website design

This website has been designed with the following features:

  • Clear headings and predictable page structure
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and visible focus indicators
  • High-contrast body text on an off-white background
  • Responsive layouts that work on mobile, tablet and desktop
  • Meaningful link text rather than repeated “click here” links
  • Alternative text for informative images and decorative images hidden from assistive technology
  • No autoplaying audio, video or rapid animation
  • Reduced motion respected through the device's accessibility preference
  • Forms with visible labels, clear required fields and plain instructions
  • Policy information available as web text as well as downloadable documents

Online sessions and communication

Reasonable adjustments can be discussed for tutoring, coaching and support services. Depending on the person and the type of session, these may include:

  • Camera-off or reduced-camera arrangements where appropriate
  • Shorter sessions, breaks or a slower pace
  • Verbal-first explanations and reduced written demands
  • Written follow-up where this has been agreed
  • Reduced visual clutter and clear step-by-step instructions
  • Extra processing time and fewer rapid questions
  • Communication by email, telephone or WhatsApp according to preference
  • Information shared with a parent, carer or professional where consent and safeguarding allow

Known limitations

Some downloadable PDF documents were created before this website and may not provide the same level of reflow or screen-reader support as the web versions. An accessible explanation or alternative format can be requested.

Third-party platforms such as Lessonspace, Google Calendar and payment providers have their own accessibility features and limitations. The MentorSphere can help explore practical alternatives where a platform creates a barrier, but cannot directly change the third-party software.

Reporting a problem

Please contact luke@thementorsphere.co.uk or telephone 07955 723 133 if:

  • You cannot access part of the website
  • You need information in a different format
  • You want to discuss an adjustment before a session
  • You find content that is confusing, inaccessible or difficult to navigate

Feedback is useful: accessibility is not a one-time checklist. Practical feedback from users will be considered when pages, documents and service systems are updated.
