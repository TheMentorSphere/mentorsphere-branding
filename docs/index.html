Why The MentorSphere is different

You should not have to explain everything from the beginning each time

A learner may first arrive for maths, then later need ADHD strategies, exam planning or support with an EHCP. The MentorSphere is designed so that the understanding built at the start can travel with them.

The aim is not to place people into separate boxes. It is to provide consistent support that recognises how learning, confidence, emotions, family life and education systems often overlap.

What you can expect

  • Calm, respectful and neurodiversity-affirming communication
  • Flexible pacing, breaks and camera use where appropriate
  • Activities linked to real interests and meaningful goals

  • Clear boundaries, safeguarding and secure online delivery
  • Honest discussion when another service would be more suitable

Support without assumptions

Specialist experience, without requiring a diagnosis

The MentorSphere specialises in neurodivergent and additional-needs support, but works with learners from all backgrounds. Every brain is different, so personalisation benefits everyone.

Sessions can be adapted around attention, sensory needs, anxiety, confidence, communication preferences and changing energy levels.
