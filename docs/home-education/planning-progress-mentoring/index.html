Home Education Support

Planning, Progress & Mentoring

Review what is working, identify gaps or overload and agree practical changes that fit the learner's goals and the family's capacity.

Discuss a reviewHome Education overview

Review the current approach

Adjust the plan before it becomes another source of pressure

Support can review curriculum choices, timetables and routines, resource selection, learner engagement, confidence and wellbeing, gaps, overload and progress records.

Learner priorities

Explore engagement, confidence, wellbeing, goals, accountability and what the learner wants to change.

Parent strategy

Agree manageable routines, responsibilities, resource choices and ways to respond when capacity changes.

Future options

Check that current choices are not unintentionally narrowing qualifications, transitions or a possible return to school.

Defined review

Curriculum & Progress Review: £175

  • Review of up to 25 pages or current records
  • 60-minute review session
  • Concise written action plan
  • Updated priorities
  • Resource suggestions
  • Agreed review date

Quoted separately

Large portfolios, multiple learners, formal report writing and detailed assessment across several subjects require a separate scope and quotation.

This is an education review, not a statutory inspection, formal accreditation or guarantee that a local authority will accept particular records.

Ongoing Home Education Mentoring: £80 per 60 minutes

Monthly or fortnightly sessions can support learner goals, reflection, parent strategy, routine and workload changes, resource adjustments and periodic plan revision.

Tailored monthly packages may be quoted where tutoring, parent support or written updates are combined.

Sustain and adapt

Keep the learner's voice central

Ongoing mentoring provides agreed review and accountability. It can help the family respond when circumstances change and coordinate direction with Tutoring or ADHD Coaching.

Direct subject teaching remains a separate tutoring service and is not included in the mentoring price.

What ongoing mentoring does not include

Ongoing mentoring does not include unlimited messaging, daily supervision, automatic document updates, unlimited professional liaison or direct academic teaching within the mentoring price.
