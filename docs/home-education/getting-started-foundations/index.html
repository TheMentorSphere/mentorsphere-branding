Getting started

Think it through before making a big decision

We can discuss how much time and energy your family has, likely costs, what needs attention first and what may make home education difficult. Your child or young person's thoughts, wishes and difficulties can be included in a way that fits their age, needs, preferences and safety.

The real issue may be unmet special educational needs and disabilities (SEND), attendance, Education Otherwise Than At School (EOTAS) or a disputed council matter. If so, I will point you towards Education & SEND Support, free help or legally based advice before you pay for a home education package.

Home Education Fit Call: Free

Up to 15 minutes to work out what kind of help you need, whether anything is urgent, which free options may help and which MentorSphere service is the right one.

Not included: detailed advice, document review, curriculum design or legal guidance.

Home Education Clarity Session: £80

  • 60-minute session
  • Limited advance intake review
  • Brief written next steps

Not included: legal advice, a recommendation to deregister, substantial document review or a complete curriculum plan.

A focused next step

Clarify what matters now

The Clarity Session is for families considering home education, those who have recently started or those who are unsure what to focus on next.

You will leave with three to five practical actions, useful free information and a clearer idea of whether to plan independently, choose the Foundations Package, use tutoring or SEND support, or take another route.

Personalised foundations

Plan the first 6 to 12 weeks around real capacity

Choose learning priorities and methods, suitable resources, a flexible weekly routine, simple progress records and a review date around your child and family.

Learning priorities and methods

Choose the most important learning areas, build on interests and look gently at where things are now before deciding what comes next.

Rhythm and resources

Select realistic resources and create a weekly rhythm that includes rest, regulation and real-life learning.

Progress and review

Keep useful progress records without too much paperwork, and agree when to review the plan or change it sooner.

A complete starting package for one learner

Home Education Foundations Package: £325

A personalised, realistic starting plan rather than a generic timetable or resource list.

  • Parent or family discovery session of up to 75 minutes
  • Optional session of up to 30 minutes to hear what the learner thinks and wants
  • Review of up to 25 pages of relevant reports, plans or current work
  • A gentle look at where learning is now, or a plan for any useful assessment
  • Curriculum priorities, learning methods and resource recommendations
  • A flexible weekly routine and a simple way to record progress

Outputs and follow-up

  • Personalised Home Education Plan, normally 5 to 8 pages
  • Priorities for the first 6 to 12 weeks
  • Suggested resources and learning routes
  • Progress and review framework
  • 45-minute follow-up session
  • One round of minor amendments within seven days of the follow-up

When you need a separate price

We will agree a separate price before starting for more than one learner, more than 25 pages, urgent work, combined Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP) or EOTAS work, substantial exam-centre research or a complex council or legal process.

Common questions

Before support begins

Will you tell me whether to deregister?

No. Support is neutral and practical. It does not provide legal advice or make that decision for a parent.

Does a plan have to follow the national curriculum?

The service can help explore structured, learner-led or blended approaches. Check current official guidance for legal responsibilities and curriculum information.

What if the situation may be EOTAS?

EOTAS is not elective home education. Use EOTAS & Education Access to explore that context.

Can the package include direct teaching?

Direct academic teaching is separate and links to Tutoring. It can be coordinated with the plan where useful.
