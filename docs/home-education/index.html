Home Education Support

Build a home education approach that works in real life

I can help you think through home education, plan the first few weeks or adjust an approach that is no longer working.

Discuss home education support Explore support

Home Education Support is part of The MentorSphere.

Who it supports

Start with your child and your family

I can help if you are considering elective home education, have recently started or have been home educating for some time and feel stuck.

Support may also help a neurodivergent child or young person who needs learning shaped around their energy, sensory needs, planning skills and confidence. Older learners can plan qualifications, college, training, work or a possible return to school.

A plan that reflects real life

  • Learner strengths, needs, interests and goals
  • Emotional and sensory needs
  • How much time and energy your family realistically has
  • Available time and realistic budgets

Home education is not automatically the right answer to every school problem. I will help you think through the questions, but I will not tell you whether to remove your child from school.

How support works

A simple process that can change with your family

1

Work out what help you need

We will check whether the issue is elective home education, EOTAS, school attendance, tutoring, SEND support or a mixture. We will also note any urgent deadline or useful free service.

2

Learn about your family

We will discuss your goals, what your child thinks and wants, their education so far, their strengths and difficulties, your available time and any resources you already use.

3

Look at where things are now

We can use existing work, gentle assessment or observation when helpful. I will avoid unnecessary testing that may add distress or make your child withdraw.

4

Make a practical plan

We will agree learning priorities, a flexible weekly routine, useful resources, responsibilities, simple progress records and a review date.

5

Put the plan into practice

Depending on what you choose, this may include parent strategies, learner mentoring, separate subject tutoring or help to keep the plan joined up.

6

Check what is working

We will change what is not helping and plan what comes next, including qualifications, college, training, work, a return to school or another route.

Home education principles

Flexible, neutral and shaped with the learner

You can use a structured, child-led or mixed approach. The plan should fit the time and energy your family has, include rest and real-life learning, and avoid copying a school day at home when that does not help.

The plan is reviewed when it stops working. Tutoring, ADHD coaching and Education & SEND Support can be coordinated where useful.

Service boundaries

Parents remain responsible for their child's education when they choose elective home education. I can help with planning, learning choices and records, but I do not take over that responsibility.

I do not provide legal advice, tell a parent whether to remove their child from school, act as an official inspector, guarantee that a council will accept a plan or record, replace clinical support, provide crisis support or offer unlimited contact.

Joined-up support

Use the right service without starting again

You can use more than one MentorSphere service without starting from the beginning each time. With your agreement, the information I already know can guide the next part of your support.

Direct subject teaching

Maths, English, science and exam preparation remain part of Tutoring.

ADHD-specific coaching

Executive function, routines and identity-focused coaching sit within ADHD Coaching.

EHCP, EOTAS and council matters

Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP) work, Education Otherwise Than At School (EOTAS) and disputed council matters sit within Education & SEND Support. Seek legally based advice where needed.
