Qualifications for home-educated learners

Plan private exams early

A private candidate is someone taking an exam without being entered through their usual school. They may need to find a suitable exam centre, check entry deadlines and understand any coursework, practical or speaking parts.

Access arrangements are changes that help someone take an exam fairly, such as extra time or a smaller room. They depend on supporting information, the learner's usual way of working and decisions made by the exam centre and awarding organisation.
