Home Education Support

Qualifications & Future Pathways

Create a realistic route towards qualifications, college, apprenticeships, training, employment, a possible return to school or another next step.

Discuss pathway planningHome Education overview

Start with the destination

Connect current learning to future options

Planning begins with the learner's goals and likely entry requirements. It can compare GCSEs, IGCSEs and Functional Skills, then consider realistic subject choices and timescales.

The roadmap can also identify coursework, practical, speaking and examination-centre requirements before choices are fixed.

Pathways to consider

  • College and sixth-form entry
  • Apprenticeships and training
  • Employment
  • A possible return to school
  • Another education route

Qualifications for home-educated learners

Plan GCSEs and other qualifications for private candidates early

Private candidates may need to identify a suitable examination centre, entry deadlines and any coursework, practical or speaking components. Access arrangements depend on evidence, normal way of working and decisions made by the relevant centre and awarding organisation.

Route comparison

Compare relevant GCSE, IGCSE and Functional Skills routes against the learner's goals and likely entry requirements.

Subjects and timescales

Select priorities and realistic timescales without overloading the learner or narrowing future options by accident.
