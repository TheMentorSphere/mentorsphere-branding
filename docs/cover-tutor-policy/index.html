Cover Tutor and Temporary Staff Policy

V1.0, effective 14 July 2025

1. Purpose

This policy explains how temporary cover may be arranged when another tutor delivers sessions on behalf of Luke Turner under The MentorSphere. Its purpose is to protect continuity, safeguarding, privacy, professionalism and the quality expected by learners and families.

2. When cover may be used

Cover may be considered for:

  • Illness, personal commitments or professional obligations
  • Planned leave or scheduled breaks
  • Temporary scaling or expansion of services
  • Short-term staffing needs during higher-demand periods

Cover is not guaranteed. A session may instead be rearranged, banked, credited or refunded according to the relevant agreement.

3. Selection of cover tutors

Cover tutors must meet appropriate standards, including:

  • Relevant teaching, tutoring or coaching experience
  • An enhanced DBS check or suitable equivalent where required
  • Understanding of safeguarding, confidentiality, neurodiversity and inclusive practice
  • Agreement to follow relevant MentorSphere policies and learner-specific guidance
  • Professional communication and reliable online delivery

A learner-specific handover is provided before the cover session, limited to information necessary for safe and effective delivery.

4. Safeguarding and data protection

Cover tutors are bound by The MentorSphere's Safeguarding Policy and Privacy Policy. They must:

  • Report safeguarding concerns immediately to Luke Turner, the Designated Safeguarding Lead
  • Keep learner information secure and confidential
  • Use only approved platforms and communication routes
  • Access only the information needed for the cover task
  • Return or securely delete temporary information when it is no longer required

5. Session format and delivery

  • Sessions take place through Lessonspace or another approved platform.
  • The established routine, pace and communication approach are followed as closely as practical.
  • Learner-specific adaptations are explained during the handover.
  • Lessonspace whiteboards and agreed records are saved after the session.
  • Recording takes place only where consent and existing arrangements allow.

6. Communication with learners and families

  • Parents, carers or adult learners are informed in advance wherever possible.
  • The temporary nature of the change is explained clearly.
  • Learners are introduced sensitively and given an opportunity to raise concerns.
  • Essential scheduling and account communication normally remains with Luke Turner unless another arrangement is stated.

7. Attendance, cancellation and rescheduling

  • The usual attendance and cancellation terms apply to a confirmed cover session.
  • A cover tutor may record non-attendance and notify Luke Turner.
  • Direct follow-up by the cover tutor occurs only where authorised.
  • Any reschedule depends on availability and the relevant booking policy.

8. Lesson summaries and transition back

The cover tutor provides a short record covering:

  • Topics or goals addressed
  • Learner engagement and any useful observations
  • Work completed or saved
  • Safeguarding, technical or attendance issues
  • Suggested next steps

Luke Turner reviews this information to support a smooth transition back to regular delivery.

9. Quality assurance and feedback

Learners and families are encouraged to share feedback about cover arrangements. Concerns should be sent to:

  • Email: luke@thementorsphere.co.uk
  • Telephone: 07955 723 133

Concerns are reviewed promptly and handled under the relevant complaints, safeguarding and privacy procedures.

10. Policy review

This policy is reviewed annually and whenever staffing, safeguarding expectations or service delivery change significantly.

Using a cover tutor does not reduce the safeguarding, confidentiality or quality standards expected across The MentorSphere.
