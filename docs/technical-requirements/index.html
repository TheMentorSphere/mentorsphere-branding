Technical Requirements and Troubleshooting

V1.1, effective 12 May 2025

1. Introduction

The MentorSphere delivers interactive online tutoring, coaching and support. A stable, accessible setup helps sessions run smoothly and reduces avoidable stress. This policy outlines minimum requirements and practical troubleshooting steps.

2. Minimum technical requirements

Device

  • A laptop, desktop computer or tablet
  • A working microphone and speakers or headphones
  • A webcam is recommended, but use can be adapted where appropriate

Smartphones are not recommended for tutoring because the smaller screen limits whiteboard and resource use.

Internet connection

  • A stable connection with at least 5 Mbps download speed
  • 10 Mbps or more is preferable for reliable audio, video and whiteboard use
  • A wired Ethernet connection is often more stable than Wi-Fi

Browser and software

  • Sessions are normally delivered through Lessonspace or another approved platform
  • Lessonspace runs in a browser and does not normally require a download
  • Use a current version of Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Safari
  • A PDF reader or browser PDF viewer may be needed for some resources

3. Recommended learning setup

  • A quiet workspace with unnecessary notifications reduced
  • Headphones with a built-in microphone where background noise is likely
  • A camera positioned at a comfortable angle if used
  • Paper and pen, or an agreed digital alternative
  • A touchscreen or stylus for direct whiteboard interaction where available

A touchscreen and stylus can improve interaction but are not essential. Learners without them can still participate fully.

4. Common issues and troubleshooting

Poor connection, freezing or lag

  • Close unnecessary apps and browser tabs
  • Disconnect devices that are using large amounts of bandwidth
  • Move closer to the router or use Ethernet
  • Turn video off temporarily if audio is breaking up
  • Restart the router if the problem continues

Audio cannot be heard

  • Check that the device and browser are not muted
  • Confirm the correct microphone and speaker are selected
  • Disconnect and reconnect headphones
  • Refresh the Lessonspace tab
  • Try a different supported browser

Camera is not working

  • Close other apps that may be using the camera
  • Check browser camera permissions
  • Refresh the page or restart the device
  • Continue by audio while the issue is investigated if appropriate

The lesson link will not open

  • Copy and paste the link into a supported browser
  • Check that the full link has been copied
  • Try a private or incognito window if the browser is using the wrong account
  • Contact The MentorSphere if the link still fails

5. Technical support during a session

For urgent technical issues at the scheduled session time:

Where a session cannot continue because of technical problems, a reschedule may be offered at The MentorSphere's discretion. The decision will take account of the cause, time lost, attempts to resolve the problem and available capacity.

6. Accessibility and alternatives

Technical requirements should not create avoidable barriers. Please raise sensory, visual, hearing, motor or communication needs before the first session where possible. Practical alternatives can be explored, although some interactive functions depend on the platform and device being used.

7. Review

This policy is reviewed when platforms or technical requirements change, and at least annually.

By joining an online session, the learner or responsible adult confirms that they have made reasonable efforts to provide a suitable device and connection, and will contact The MentorSphere promptly if a problem prevents access.
