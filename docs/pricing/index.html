How monthly subscription invoices are calculated

The subscription table shows representative prices based on the standard number of sessions included within each plan.

The actual number of scheduled sessions may vary between calendar months. For example, a customer receiving one session per week may have four sessions in one billing month and five in another.

Where the billing month contains additional or fewer scheduled sessions, the invoice will be adjusted using the applicable discounted per-session rate for the customer's selected subscription plan, session length and session frequency. The same plan-based per-session rate will be used when adding or deducting sessions.

The applicable per-session rate is calculated by dividing the displayed standard plan price by the standard number of sessions included within that plan:

  • Essentials: 2 sessions
  • Confidence Builder: 4 sessions
  • Skills Booster: 8 sessions
  • Fast-track: 12 sessions

The final invoice total is rounded to the nearest penny.

Worked example: the displayed price for four 60-minute Confidence Builder sessions is £150. Dividing £150 by four gives a discounted per-session rate of £37.50. If five 60-minute sessions fall within the billing month, the invoice is £187.50.

Booking, cancellation and rescheduling

Current terms in plain English

The prices and terms shown on this page apply to current bookings. Where a service is bespoke or requires substantial preparation, any different pricing or cancellation arrangements will be confirmed in writing before work begins. Please contact The MentorSphere if you need clarification about how any term applies to your circumstances.

Cancellation and rescheduling

The standard notice period for cancelling or rescheduling a session is 48 hours.

The MentorSphere recognises that disability, neurodivergence, illness, family circumstances and unexpected events can sometimes make advance notice difficult. Requests received with less than 48 hours' notice will therefore be considered reasonably and flexibly wherever possible.

The MentorSphere will always try to offer a suitable replacement time so that the customer does not lose the session. However, a replacement session cannot be guaranteed, particularly where little notice is provided or suitable availability cannot be found.

PAYG and prepaid sessions

Where at least 48 hours' notice is provided, a PAYG or prepaid session can normally be rearranged or cancelled without charge.

Where less than 48 hours' notice is provided, The MentorSphere will endeavour to rearrange the session.

If a suitable replacement time cannot reasonably be arranged, the session may remain chargeable or be treated as used.

No separate standard late-cancellation fee will be added on top of the session charge.

Subscription sessions

Subscription payments reserve regular session availability for the relevant billing month.

Subscription fees are not normally refundable once the relevant billing month has begun. This does not apply where a refund is required by law or where The MentorSphere is unable to provide the agreed service.

Where a subscription session is cancelled or missed at short notice, The MentorSphere will endeavour to rearrange it so that the customer still receives the session they have paid for.

A replacement session should normally be used within 60 days of the original session date.

Additional flexibility may be agreed where appropriate, including during prolonged illness, disability-related circumstances, significant family difficulties or other exceptional situations.

Where a suitable replacement cannot reasonably be arranged, the session may be treated as used.

Cancelling or changing a subscription plan

Customers may request to cancel or change a subscription plan at any time by providing written notice.

To prevent the next scheduled payment from being collected, notice must be received at least 10 working days before the next payment date.

Where notice is received less than 10 working days before the next payment date, the upcoming payment may still be collected and the cancellation or change will normally take effect from the following billing cycle.

Sessions already included within a paid billing month remain subject to the subscription-session terms above.

Bespoke support services

Some support services may involve substantial preparation, document review, report writing, research, coordination, booking arrangements or other work before a scheduled appointment or final service is delivered.

Any service-specific cancellation arrangements should be explained to the customer in writing before work begins wherever reasonably possible.

Where a customer cancels after work has already begun, The MentorSphere may retain or charge a reasonable amount reflecting:

  • Work already completed
  • Time already committed
  • Non-recoverable third-party costs
  • Direct losses resulting from the cancellation

Any amount retained or charged must be proportionate to the work completed and costs incurred. It must not duplicate a loss that has already been covered by another charge.

Cancellations by The MentorSphere

If The MentorSphere has to cancel a session, the customer will be offered:

  • A replacement session
  • An appropriate account credit
  • A refund where a suitable replacement cannot be provided

Nothing in these terms affects the customer's statutory rights.

Can a plan combine subjects?

Yes. A learner can alternate subjects, split a longer session or change focus around current priorities. The schedule and price are agreed clearly.

Can siblings or more than one learner be included?

Family and multi-learner arrangements can be discussed. The structure depends on whether sessions are shared or separate and on the planning involved.

Do monthly plans charge for school holidays?

Invoices reflect the sessions that are actually scheduled. Planned breaks, banked sessions and changes are handled under the agreed booking terms.

Are there hidden charges?

No substantive additional work should begin without agreement. Optional reports, extensive marking, bespoke document work or other extras are discussed and priced first.

Need a plan that does not fit neatly into a table?

Mixed subjects, changing frequencies and combined support can be built around the learner. The free introduction is the easiest place to discuss it.
