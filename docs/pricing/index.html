Booking and cancellation summary

Key terms in plain English

The full pricing policy remains the formal source. These points provide a quick overview.

PAYG sessions

  • Payment is required when the session is booked.
  • More than 48 hours' notice: reschedule or refund can normally be offered.
  • Less than 48 hours' notice: a £25 late cancellation charge may apply.
  • No attendance or contact within 15 minutes is treated as a late cancellation.

Monthly plans

  • Plans run monthly without a long-term contract.
  • Sessions can normally be rearranged with at least 48 hours' notice.
  • Pre-notified unused sessions may be banked for up to 30 days.
  • Changes to frequency or cancellation should be requested before the next billing cycle.
View all policies
Can a plan combine subjects?

Yes. A learner can alternate subjects, split a longer session or change focus around current priorities. The schedule and price are agreed clearly.

Can siblings or more than one learner be included?

Family and multi-learner arrangements can be discussed. The structure depends on whether sessions are shared or separate and on the planning involved.

Do monthly plans charge for school holidays?

Invoices reflect the sessions that are actually scheduled. Planned breaks, banked sessions and changes are handled under the agreed booking terms.

Are there hidden charges?

No substantive additional work should begin without agreement. Optional reports, extensive marking, bespoke document work or other extras are discussed and priced first.

Need a plan that does not fit neatly into a table?

Mixed subjects, changing frequencies and combined support can be built around the learner. The free introduction is the easiest place to discuss it.
