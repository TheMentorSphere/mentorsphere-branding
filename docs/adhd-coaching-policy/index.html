ADHD Coaching Policy

V1.0, effective 1 May 2025

1. Introduction

This policy explains the structure, expectations and terms of ADHD coaching delivered through The ADHD Mentor, a branch of The MentorSphere. It applies to adults, young people, parents and Access to Work clients.

The approach is informed by lived experience, professional practice, practical strategies and a neurodiversity-affirming understanding of ADHD.

2. Scope of coaching

ADHD coaching is proactive, reflective and strategy-based. It can support areas such as:

  • Routines, planning and everyday executive function
  • Emotional regulation and recovery after difficult moments
  • Motivation, procrastination and task initiation
  • School, work, study and relationships
  • Identity, confidence and self-understanding
  • Parent understanding and family strategies

Coaching is not psychotherapy, clinical diagnosis, medical treatment or crisis support. It may complement these services but does not replace them.

3. Coaching pathways

  • Young people: emotional tools, identity, routines, school stress and growing independence.
  • Adults: work, study, relationships, motivation, self-understanding and executive function.
  • Parents and carers: understanding behaviour, supporting regulation, building routines and navigating systems.
  • Access to Work: workplace strategies, agreed records and reports within the funding arrangement.

4. Session delivery

  • Sessions are delivered online using an approved platform such as Lessonspace or Google Meet.
  • Sessions are normally 60 minutes unless another arrangement is agreed.
  • A verbal-first model is used to reduce unnecessary admin and overwhelm.
  • Internal notes are kept securely. Detailed written summaries or reports are supplied only where agreed and may carry an additional fee.
  • Adjustments such as camera-off arrangements, breaks or shorter sessions can be discussed where appropriate.

Technical expectations are explained in the Technical Requirements and Troubleshooting Policy.

5. Booking and attendance

  • Sessions are booked through the approved booking link or direct arrangement.
  • At least 24 hours' notice is requested for coaching rescheduling unless a different written term applies to the booking.
  • A missed session without notice may be treated as used.
  • Where a package is purchased, any validity period is confirmed before payment.

6. Fees and packages

Current prices are published on the pricing page and confirmed before booking. If an older policy PDF contains a previous fee, the latest fee confirmed in writing will apply.

Payments are normally required in advance. Access to Work rates, reports, packages or additional message support may be priced differently from a standard private session.

7. Confidentiality and safeguarding

Coaching is private, but not absolutely confidential. Information may be shared where there is a serious risk of harm, a safeguarding concern, a legal requirement or a need agreed by the client.

Young people are given age-appropriate privacy. Parent communication and the limits of confidentiality are discussed at the start. The Safeguarding Policy applies throughout.

8. Inclusion and neurodiversity

The service is LGBTQIA+ affirmative, trauma-aware and recovery-informed. It aims to avoid shame-based, compliance-led or one-size-fits-all approaches. The SEND, SEMH and Neurodiversity Policy provides further information.

9. Access to Work coaching

  • Funding and approved support should be confirmed before sessions begin.
  • Session records or reports are prepared according to the award and service agreement.
  • Coaching may be paused where funding approval or payment is delayed.
  • The client remains responsible for understanding the terms of their Access to Work award.

10. Feedback and complaints

Informal feedback is welcome. Concerns should be raised with Luke Turner as soon as possible so that they can be understood and addressed. Safeguarding concerns are managed under the Safeguarding Policy.

11. Policy review

This policy is reviewed annually, and earlier where service delivery, legislation, safeguarding expectations or professional practice change.

Engaging with The ADHD Mentor confirms agreement to the coaching scope, boundaries and booking terms that have been provided for the specific service.
