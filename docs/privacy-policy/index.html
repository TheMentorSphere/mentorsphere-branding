Privacy Policy

V1.3, effective 28 July 2026

1. Introduction

The MentorSphere is committed to protecting personal information and privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, protected and disclosed when someone engages with tutoring, coaching, support services, the website or associated platforms.

2. Information collected

The MentorSphere may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Personal information: name, email address, telephone number and other contact details.
  • Payment information: processed securely by trusted third-party providers such as GoCardless or Stripe.
  • Technical information: information generated through the website, booking system and online platforms, such as browser type, device usage or IP address where applicable.
  • Educational information: learning preferences, session notes, baseline assessments, educational background and other information used to tailor support.
  • Recording consent information: where permission has been granted, records of consent relating to audio, video, screen sharing or whiteboard recording.

3. How information is used

Personal information may be used to:

  • Deliver and personalise tutoring, coaching and support services
  • Manage scheduling and communication
  • Process payments securely
  • Record sessions where explicit consent has been provided
  • Improve services using appropriate feedback and engagement information
  • Meet legal, safeguarding and professional obligations

Recordings are stored securely, are not shared externally and are retained only for the agreed educational, safeguarding or quality purpose.

4. Information sharing and security

  • Personal data is not sold or rented to third parties.
  • Information is shared with trusted third parties only where needed to deliver the service, process payments or meet legal and safeguarding duties.
  • Appropriate technical and organisational measures are used to protect information.
  • The MentorSphere works in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

5. Individual rights

Under UK GDPR, individuals may have the following rights, subject to relevant legal and safeguarding exceptions:

  • Right of access: request a copy of personal data.
  • Right to rectification: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure: request deletion where there is no lawful reason to retain the data.
  • Right to restrict processing: request limits on how information is used.
  • Right to object: object to processing in specific circumstances.
  • Right to data portability: request data in a commonly used, structured format where the right applies.

To exercise a right or raise a concern, email luke@thementorsphere.co.uk.

6. Third-party services

Services used to support delivery may include:

  • Lessonspace: live online tutoring and interactive whiteboards.
  • Google Calendar and Google Forms: scheduling and learner intake.
  • GoCardless or Stripe: secure payment processing.
  • Calendly or Google appointment scheduling: booking introductory sessions.
  • Formspree: processing and delivering enquiries submitted through the website contact form.

Third-party services are selected with data protection and service suitability in mind. They operate under their own privacy terms.

7. Session recordings

  • Recordings are made only with explicit prior consent.
  • A recording may include video, audio, screen sharing and interactive whiteboard activity.
  • Recordings are used only for safeguarding, training, quality or educational review.
  • Recordings are stored securely for up to 12 months unless a different retention period is justified by safeguarding or legal need.
  • Consent can be withdrawn by contacting luke@thementorsphere.co.uk. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before it was withdrawn.

8. Website contact form

The website enquiry form is processed by Formspree on behalf of The MentorSphere. When the form is submitted, Formspree processes the information entered into the form so that the enquiry can be delivered to The MentorSphere and managed within the Formspree account.

The information submitted may include:

  • Name and email address
  • Telephone number and preferred contact method, where provided
  • The selected area of support
  • The enquiry message and privacy acknowledgement
  • The contact page address used as the source of the enquiry

This information is used to respond to the enquiry and manage a possible service relationship. It is not sold. Visitors should not use the initial form to send detailed medical records, identification documents or other highly sensitive personal information. Secure ways to share further information can be discussed after contact has been established.

Formspree operates under its own privacy terms. Read the Formspree Privacy Policy for information about how Formspree handles personal data.

9. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when services, platforms, legal requirements or data practices change. Material changes may be communicated through the website, email or booking platform.
