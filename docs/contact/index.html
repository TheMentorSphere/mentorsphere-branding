Not sure what to write?

Three points are enough for a first message

Include who the support is for, the main thing that is difficult at the moment, and what you hope support might change. You can add documents and detail later if needed.

Example: “We are looking for support for our 13-year-old. School and home routines have become difficult, and we are unsure whether tutoring, ADHD coaching or education and SEND support is the best starting point.”

Helpful details, where relevant

  • Age and current stage of education, where relevant
  • Subject, qualification or support area
  • Known or suspected additional needs
  • What has already been tried
  • Preferred days, times and session frequency
  • Any decision, meeting or exam deadline

Urgent help
