Private candidates and exam preparation

Private exams and access arrangement support

Guidance with private exam entry, examination centres, supporting evidence and preparation for access arrangement discussions.

Discuss exam support See what support can include

Examination centres and awarding organisations make entry and access arrangement decisions. The MentorSphere cannot guarantee approval or availability.

Who it may help

Practical coordination for learners entering exams privately

This service may help home-educated learners, EOTAS learners, adults returning to learning and other private candidates who need to organise exam entry or prepare access arrangement information.

Support focuses on understanding the practical route, identifying questions and bringing relevant information together before deadlines.

Possible starting points

  • Uncertainty about private candidate entry
  • Questions to ask an examination centre
  • Several subjects, dates or requirements to organise
  • Supporting evidence for access arrangement discussions
  • A need to coordinate learning and exam preparation

What support can include

Organise the questions, evidence and next steps

Private exam entry

Clarify the intended qualifications, subjects, likely dates and practical questions to raise with potential examination centres.

Access arrangement preparation

Organise existing reports, examples of need, normal ways of working and questions about the centre's evidence process.

Coordination and planning

Create a clear action list for enquiries, documents, deadlines, learning preparation and agreed follow-up.

Useful information to bring

  • The qualifications and subjects being considered
  • Preferred exam series or important dates
  • Any centres already contacted
  • Relevant reports or assessment information
  • Examples of the learner's normal way of working
  • Questions, concerns or previous arrangements

If some details are unknown, identifying the right questions can be the first part of the support.

Clear evidence

Prepare without assuming an outcome

Access arrangement preparation can help present relevant information clearly, but it does not decide what evidence a centre will accept or what arrangement will be approved.

Centres may have their own processes, capacity and deadlines. Contacting them early can make practical options easier to understand.

Pricing

Quoted according to the coordination required

The initial discussion is free. A defined task is quoted in advance, while support involving several subjects, centres, documents or stages receives a bespoke quotation.

Complexity, deadlines, document volume and the expected output affect the fee. No substantive paid work begins before the scope and price are agreed.

What the service cannot guarantee

The MentorSphere cannot guarantee centre availability, entry acceptance, access arrangements, qualification results or deadlines set by other organisations.

The service does not carry out a clinical diagnosis, make an awarding decision or replace advice from the relevant examination centre or awarding organisation.

Common questions

Before exam support begins

Can you guarantee a private exam place?

No. Availability and entry decisions remain with examination centres. Support can help organise enquiries and the information needed to compare practical options.

Can you approve access arrangements?

No. The relevant centre and awarding process determine what evidence is required and whether an arrangement is approved. Support can help prepare information and questions.

Can support include exam preparation tutoring?

Yes, where tutoring is suitable and agreed. Learning and tutoring is a separate pathway that can work alongside practical exam coordination.

What if the entry deadline is close?

Include the exact qualification, subject, exam series and deadline in your first message. Urgent work depends on capacity and centre availability.

Clarify the practical exam route

Share the qualification, subject, intended exam series and any access arrangement questions or deadlines already known.
