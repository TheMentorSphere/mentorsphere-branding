Before referral and assessment conversations

ADHD and autism referral preparation

I can help you bring everyday examples together and prepare questions for a GP, Right to Choose or assessment conversation.

Discuss referral preparation See what support can include

The MentorSphere does not diagnose ADHD or autism, make referrals, provide medical advice or guarantee access to an assessment.

Who it may help

Bring the important examples together

This service may help adults, parents, families and young people who want to prepare before speaking with a GP, education professional or assessment provider.

I can help you explain what daily life is like in your own words. We will not change information or practise answers to try to reach a particular result.

Possible starting points

  • Screening forms that feel difficult to complete
  • Examples spread across home, education or work
  • Uncertainty about what information may be relevant
  • Questions about a GP or assessment conversation
  • Documents and reports that need organising

What support can include

Present the person's experience clearly and honestly

Organise examples

Bring together examples from everyday life, education, work, relationships, routines, emotions, planning and organisation.

Work through information

Talk through screening questions, identify useful documents and record answers in the person's own words.

Prepare conversations

Create a concise summary, list of questions and practical plan for a GP, Right to Choose or assessment discussion.

Useful information to bring

  • Examples from childhood and current life where available
  • Patterns across home, education, work or relationships
  • Previous screening information
  • Relevant school, workplace or clinical documents
  • Strategies already tried and their effect
  • Questions or concerns about the next conversation

Not every person will have all of this information. The service can help identify useful gaps without treating missing records as proof that a need is absent.

Lived experience in context

Explain strengths as well as difficulties

Preparation can include what the person does well, what they find difficult and the difference this makes in daily life. The aim is to help the right professional understand their experience and why they are asking for a further conversation.

I use neurodiversity-affirming language, but I do not assume what an assessment will find or speak for every neurodivergent person.

Pricing

ADHD or Autism Referral Preparation: £100

This includes a 60-minute session, help to organise relevant examples, a preparation checklist and questions for a GP or assessment provider.

We will agree a separate price before starting for a detailed written summary, emails or letters to a provider, more than one family or professional meeting, or ongoing contact.

What the service cannot guarantee

The MentorSphere cannot diagnose, make a medical referral, produce a clinical report, confirm screening conclusions or coach someone towards particular answers.

It cannot guarantee a referral, provider choice, assessment, waiting time or diagnostic outcome. Referral and assessment decisions remain with the appropriate healthcare professional or provider.

Common questions

Before referral preparation begins

Can you diagnose ADHD or autism?

No. This is a preparation and organisation service. Only an appropriately qualified clinical professional can complete a diagnostic assessment.

Will you complete screening forms for me?

Support can help explain questions, organise examples and record information clearly, but answers must remain accurate to the person completing the form.

Can you guarantee a Right to Choose referral?

No. Support can help prepare information and questions, but eligibility, referral and provider decisions sit with the relevant healthcare professionals and services.

Do I need all my childhood records?

No. Bring what is available. Preparation can identify helpful examples and gaps, but missing records are handled honestly and are not replaced with assumptions.

Prepare the information in your own words

Tell me which conversation or form you are preparing for, any deadline and what feels difficult to organise.
