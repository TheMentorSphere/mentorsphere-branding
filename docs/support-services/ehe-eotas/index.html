Home education or EOTAS: choose the right route

Elective home education and EOTAS are different arrangements. Use the route that matches the learner's current situation.

Elective home education

Home Education Support

For families considering, starting or reviewing a parent-led home education approach, including curriculum, progress, routines and future pathways.

Go to Home Education Support

Education arranged outside school

EOTAS & Education Access

For practical preparation where a learner cannot currently access suitable education through a standard school arrangement, or an EOTAS package is being considered or reviewed.

Go to EOTAS & Education Access

Unsure which situation applies?

The initial discussion can clarify the route and identify appropriate free or legally based information. The MentorSphere does not provide legal advice or treat EOTAS and elective home education as interchangeable.
