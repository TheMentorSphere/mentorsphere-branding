Education, Health and Care Plans

EHCP support that helps you prepare clearly

Practical help to understand the EHCP process, organise evidence and prepare for requests, reviews, meetings or next steps.

Discuss EHCP support See what support can include

The MentorSphere provides education information and preparation support. It does not provide legal representation or make statutory decisions.

Who it may help

Support when the process feels difficult to organise

EHCP support may help parents, families and young people who need to understand where they are in the process, bring information together or prepare for an important conversation.

You do not need to arrive with every document in order. The first step can be identifying what is available, what is missing and what needs attention first.

Possible starting points

  • Preparing information for an EHC needs assessment request
  • Organising evidence for a review
  • Understanding paperwork or a response
  • Preparing questions for a meeting
  • Clarifying practical next steps

What support can include

Clear preparation without taking over your story

Understand the stage

Explain the current situation in plain English, identify important dates and separate immediate actions from later questions.

Organise the evidence

Bring together reports, school information, examples of need, current support and the impact when support is not available.

Prepare communications

Develop a request, evidence summary, list of questions, meeting plan or clear follow-up actions within the agreed scope.

Useful information to bring

  • Key letters, emails or decisions
  • School or setting information
  • Professional reports or assessment summaries
  • Examples of needs and everyday impact
  • Support already tried and what happened
  • Important dates, meetings or deadlines

Incomplete information is not a barrier to making contact. Identifying what is missing can be part of the support.

Person-centred evidence

Show need, impact and the support required

Useful evidence is more than a diagnosis or test score. It can show strengths, barriers, support needs and what happens when the right support is absent.

The language remains respectful and neurodiversity-affirming while being specific enough to support clear communication.

Pricing

Quoted around the task and documents involved

The initial discussion is free. A defined task is quoted in advance, while support involving several documents or stages receives a bespoke quotation.

Complexity, deadlines, document volume and the expected output affect the fee. No substantive paid work begins before the scope and price are agreed.

What the service cannot guarantee

The MentorSphere cannot guarantee an assessment, plan, amendment, placement, funding decision or appeal outcome. Decisions remain with the relevant local authority, setting or tribunal.

The service does not provide legal representation or replace independent legal or statutory advice.

Common questions

Before EHCP support begins

Can you write an EHCP request for me?

Support can help organise evidence and prepare clear wording within an agreed scope. The information must accurately reflect the child or young person and the evidence available.

Can you attend an EHCP meeting?

Meeting preparation is available. Attendance may be possible in selected circumstances and must be agreed separately according to purpose, availability, role boundaries and cost.

Can you guarantee that a request or review will succeed?

No. Support can improve organisation and clarity, but decisions are made by the relevant statutory body and no outcome can be guaranteed.

What if an appeal or legal deadline is involved?

Share the exact deadline immediately. The MentorSphere does not provide legal representation, and independent specialist legal advice may also be needed.

Start by explaining where you are in the process

Include any important date or deadline. The introductory discussion can clarify the information available and the most useful next step.
