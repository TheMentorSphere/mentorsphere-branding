The Support Mentor

Clear, practical help through education and SEND systems

Support to organise information, understand the next step and communicate needs more clearly. The aim is to reduce overwhelm while keeping the learner's voice, strengths and real circumstances at the centre.

Families and learners are often asked to navigate complicated processes while already managing stress, uncertainty and competing needs. The Support Mentor offers structured help without taking over the person's story.

A structured process

From a large, tangled problem to manageable actions

You do not need to arrive with every document organised or know the correct terminology. The opening conversation is used to identify what has happened, what is urgent and what information is still missing.

Where the work is likely to take more than one appointment, you will receive a clear proposed scope and price before continuing.

  1. 1. Understand the situation: goals, history, current support and immediate concerns.
  2. 2. Organise the evidence: identify useful documents, examples, assessments and missing information.
  3. 3. Clarify the options: explain possible next steps in plain English, including limitations and uncertainties.
  4. 4. Prepare the action: draft, plan, checklist, meeting questions or referral information.
  5. 5. Review what happens next: follow-up support can be added if the situation changes or a response arrives.

Useful information to bring

  • Key letters, emails or decisions
  • Reports or assessment summaries
  • Examples of needs and their everyday impact
  • Support already tried and what happened
  • Important dates or deadlines
  • The outcome you hope to achieve

Do not delay contacting The MentorSphere just because this information is incomplete. Identifying what is missing can be part of the support.

Person-centred evidence

Clear paperwork without losing the human story

Good evidence should explain more than a diagnosis or test score. It should show what the learner can do, where barriers arise, what support is needed and what happens when that support is absent.

The language remains respectful and neurodiversity-affirming while still being specific about need and impact.

Important professional boundaries

The Support Mentor can provide education information, mentoring, document support and practical preparation. It does not provide legal representation, clinical diagnosis, medical advice or a guaranteed outcome from a school, local authority, examination centre or healthcare provider. Where specialist legal, medical or statutory advice is needed, appropriate independent services should also be considered.

Common questions

Before you request support

Can you diagnose ADHD or autism?

No. The MentorSphere does not provide a clinical diagnosis. Support can help you organise examples, complete screening information and prepare for a referral or assessment pathway.

Can you guarantee that an EHCP request or appeal will succeed?

No. Decisions are made by the relevant authority or tribunal. The service can help present information clearly, identify evidence and prepare practical next steps, but cannot guarantee an outcome.

Can you attend meetings?

Meeting preparation is available. Attendance may be possible in selected circumstances and must be agreed separately, taking account of availability, role boundaries and cost.

Can you work alongside a tutor, school, SENDCo or clinician?

Yes, where the learner or parent has provided appropriate consent. The aim is to improve continuity, not duplicate or replace another professional's role.

What happens if my deadline is very close?

Include the exact deadline in your first message. Urgent work depends on capacity and may not always be possible. Where appropriate, you may be signposted to statutory or specialist services that can respond more directly.

You do not need to know the name of the service you need

Explain what is happening in ordinary language. The introductory conversation can be used to clarify the problem, the deadline and the most useful next step.
