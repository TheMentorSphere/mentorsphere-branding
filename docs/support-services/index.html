Important professional boundaries

The MentorSphere does not provide legal representation, clinical diagnosis, medical advice or guaranteed outcomes. Support can help with education information, organisation, preparation and communication, but it does not replace legal, clinical or statutory decision-making.

Common questions

Before you request support

Can you diagnose ADHD or autism?

No. The MentorSphere does not provide clinical diagnosis. The referral preparation service can help organise examples, screening information and questions for an appropriate professional.

Can you guarantee an EHCP, exam arrangement or referral outcome?

No. Decisions are made by the relevant local authority, examination centre, awarding organisation or healthcare provider. Support can help present information clearly and prepare practical next steps.

Can you work alongside a school, SENDCo, tutor or clinician?

Yes, where it fits the agreed support and appropriate consent has been provided. The aim is to improve continuity without duplicating or replacing another professional's role.

What if my deadline is very close?

Include the exact deadline in your first message. Urgent work depends on capacity and may not always be possible. Where appropriate, other statutory or specialist services may be suggested.

You do not need to know the name of the service you need

Explain what is happening in ordinary language. The introductory conversation can clarify the problem, deadline and most useful next step.
