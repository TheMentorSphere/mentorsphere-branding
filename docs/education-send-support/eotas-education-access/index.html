Education & SEND Support

EOTAS & Education Access

Practical preparation where a learner cannot currently access suitable education through a standard school arrangement, or where an EOTAS or alternative package is being considered.

Discuss education access Education & SEND overview

EOTAS and elective home education are different arrangements.

Clarify the practical position

Support can clarify the learner's current arrangement, immediate barriers, why existing provision may not be working and relevant education, SEND and access needs. It can help prepare for package discussions, review whether a proposed package seems practically workable, and support education timetables, transitions and coordination.

It can also identify questions that need legal or statutory advice.

Fixed starting points

Initial Strategy Session: £80
A focused starting point where the immediate issue can be addressed without substantial document work.

Document Review & Strategy Session: £150
Up to 25 pages, a 60-minute consultation and brief written action plan.

Legal and statutory boundaries

EOTAS request preparation, proposed-package preparation, multi-agency planning, large evidence reviews, meeting attendance, professional liaison and ongoing coordination are quoted separately.

The MentorSphere cannot determine legal entitlement or require a local authority to agree or fund provision. Appeals, enforceability and disputed statutory duties need appropriately legally based support. Consider free independent information from SENDIASS or IPSEA.
