Education & SEND Support

Meetings, Evidence & Communication

Focused preparation so you can enter important meetings with clear priorities, usable evidence and a realistic plan for what should happen next.

Discuss meeting support Education & SEND overview

Practical preparation and follow-up

Support can cover SEND, annual review, attendance, reintegration and transition meetings. It can help clarify desired outcomes and create agendas, question lists, evidence summaries, learner profiles, parent or learner views, education histories, draft letters and reasonable-adjustment requests.

Remote attendance, post-meeting action summaries and professional liaison may be available with consent and an agreed scope.

Packages

Meeting Preparation Package: £160
Review of up to 25 pages, 60-minute preparation session and personalised agenda or question list.

Remote Meeting Support Package: £275
Everything in the preparation package, remote attendance up to 90 minutes and a brief post-meeting action summary.

Additional meeting time: £40 per 30 minutes
Subject to availability.

Follow-up Support: £45 / £80
30 / 60 minutes on an existing matter.

Attendance boundaries

In-person attendance, travel, mediation, tribunal-related work, extensive drafting, multiple revisions, meetings beyond the included allowance, ongoing liaison and case management are quoted separately.

Attendance is as an education support professional, not legal representation. Action summaries are not certified transcripts. Liaison requires consent and a defined scope.
