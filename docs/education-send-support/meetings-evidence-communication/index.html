Practical preparation and follow-up

Support can cover SEND, annual review, attendance, reintegration and transition meetings. It can help clarify desired outcomes and create agendas, question lists, evidence summaries, learner profiles, parent or learner views, education histories, draft letters and reasonable-adjustment requests.

Remote attendance, post-meeting action summaries and professional liaison may be available with consent and an agreed scope.
