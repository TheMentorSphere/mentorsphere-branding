What support can include

Understanding the SEND or EHCP stage, identifying deadlines and preparing an EHC needs assessment request. Support can also include reviewing reports and school information, organising evidence, identifying unclear wording or gaps, annual review preparation, questions, and agreed correspondence.

The focus is on describing needs, impact, outcomes and provision clearly and honestly.

Quoted separately and important boundaries

Full EHC needs assessment request preparation, large evidence bundles, detailed EHCP comparisons, amendment recommendations, urgent or legal-deadline work and ongoing case coordination are quoted separately.

This is education information and preparation, not legal advice or representation. Evidence is organised, never manufactured, and no assessment, plan, funding, placement or appeal outcome can be guaranteed. Free legal and statutory signposting is provided where appropriate.
