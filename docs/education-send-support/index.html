Education & SEND Support

Clear, practical help through education and SEND processes

Understand your situation, organise evidence, prepare for meetings, communicate concerns and turn complicated next steps into manageable actions.

Discuss your situation Explore support

The Support Mentor is a specialist visual identity within The MentorSphere's joined-up education and neurodiversity support service.

Who it is for

Support shaped around the current situation

This service may help families navigating SEND and EHCP processes, learners whose education is not working, parents preparing for reviews, families exploring EOTAS or alternative education arrangements, and private candidates planning qualifications.

It can also help when evidence, correspondence or next steps feel difficult to organise.

If the need is parent-led elective home education planning rather than EOTAS or a statutory process, visit Home Education Support.

What support can do

  • Clarify the stage, priorities and deadlines
  • Organise useful information and evidence
  • Prepare questions, meetings and communications
  • Identify practical next steps and appropriate signposting

Pricing snapshot

Focused support starts from £80

Straightforward consultations and selected review services have clear fixed prices. Work involving extensive evidence, document preparation, liaison, meeting attendance or ongoing involvement is quoted in advance.

View Education & SEND pricing

Free and independent support

You may not need to pay for support. Where a free, independent or statutory service is likely to meet your needs, I will explain that option clearly.

I charge for tailored time, preparation and defined outputs, not simply to repeat information an appropriate free service can provide.

Find local SENDIASS, IPSEA, Contact and GOV.UK education guidance may help.

Service boundaries

Education & SEND Support does not provide legal advice, tribunal representation, clinical diagnosis, medical advice, therapy, crisis support, statutory decision-making or approval of access arrangements. It cannot guarantee EHCP, EOTAS, examination, funding or placement outcomes. Communication and case-management support are only provided within an agreed scope.
