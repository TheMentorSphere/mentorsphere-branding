Education & SEND Support

Practical help with education and SEND

I can help you understand what is happening, prepare for conversations and work out what to do next.

Discuss your situation Explore support

Education & SEND Support is part of The MentorSphere.

Who it is for

Start with what is happening now

SEND means special educational needs and disabilities. An EHCP is an Education, Health and Care Plan. I can help when your child or young person's education is not working, when an EHCP request or review feels confusing, or when you need to prepare for an important meeting.

I can also help you read reports, organise useful information, prepare questions and explain your child or young person's needs clearly.

EOTAS means Education Otherwise Than At School. It is different from elective home education. If you are choosing and planning parent-led home education, visit Home Education Support.

What support can do

  • Understand the situation and any deadlines
  • Read reports and organise useful information
  • Prepare questions, meetings, emails and letters
  • Make a clear list of your next steps

Additional specialist support

Help that can sit alongside the main services

I can also help you prepare information for an ADHD or autism referral conversation. With your permission, agreed work can also include speaking with professionals, preparing a learner profile or bringing together tutoring, coaching and SEND support.

1

Tell me what is happening

We will work out what kind of help you need, whether anything is urgent and whether a free service may be a better first step.

View Education & SEND pricing

Free and independent support

You may not need to pay for support. Where a free, independent or statutory service is likely to meet your needs, I will explain that option clearly.

I charge for personal preparation and agreed written work. I will not charge simply to repeat information that a suitable free service can provide.

Find local SENDIASS, IPSEA, Contact and GOV.UK education guidance may help.

Service boundaries

I can help you prepare and organise information. I cannot give legal advice, act as a solicitor or legal representative, diagnose a condition, make decisions for a school or council, or provide emergency or crisis support.

I cannot guarantee an assessment, EHCP, EOTAS arrangement, placement, funding or exam arrangement. Any contact with professionals is agreed in advance and only happens with your permission.
