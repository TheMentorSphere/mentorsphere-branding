Education & SEND Support

Private Exams & Access Arrangements

I can help you compare qualification routes, prepare questions for exam centres and plan the practical steps from subject choice to exam day.

Discuss private exam planning Education & SEND overview

Plan each step early

A private candidate is someone taking an exam without being entered through their usual school. I can help you compare GCSE, IGCSE and Functional Skills routes, check likely timelines and practical requirements, and prepare questions for exam centres.

Access arrangements are changes that help someone take an exam fairly, such as extra time or a smaller room. I can help you organise existing information about the learner's usual way of working and prepare for an access-arrangement request. The exam centre and awarding organisation decide whether an arrangement is approved.

Where useful, private-exam planning can be coordinated with Tutoring and the wider education plan. Home-educating families can also use Qualifications & Future Pathways for a broader roadmap.

Fixed planning session

Private Candidate Planning Session: £100
A 60-minute session to compare qualification routes and practical requirements, followed by a written action checklist.

We will agree a separate price before starting if you want me to research or contact several centres, or handle booking or centre administration. The same applies to detailed access information, ongoing contact with a centre or a combined exam and tuition programme.

Who makes the decisions

Exam centres decide whether they can accept a private candidate. Centres and awarding organisations decide access arrangements. I cannot approve an arrangement or guarantee a centre place, a deadline or an outcome.
