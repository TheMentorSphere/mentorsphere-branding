Safeguarding Policy

Protecting learners in online education and support

This web version reflects Safeguarding Policy V1.2, effective from 8 September 2025.

Report a concern

The MentorSphere

Safeguarding Policy

V1.2, effective 8 September 2025

Immediate danger: call 999. This website and business contact number are not monitored as emergency services.

1. Introduction

The MentorSphere is committed to safeguarding and protecting the welfare of all learners. Safeguarding is everyone's responsibility, and concerns must be recognised, recorded and acted upon promptly.

2. Policy aims

  • Protect learners from harm and abuse
  • Set out clear responsibilities and reporting procedures
  • Maintain a safe, inclusive and respectful online learning environment
  • Respond proportionately and without unnecessary delay

3. Roles and responsibilities

Designated Safeguarding Lead: Luke Turner

  • Email: luke@thementorsphere.co.uk
  • Telephone: 07955 723 133

Useful national contacts:

  • NSPCC Helpline: 0808 800 5000
  • Childline: 0800 1111
  • Emergency services: 999

Local safeguarding services should be contacted through the relevant local authority's Children's Services.

4. Recognising signs of abuse or harm

Possible indicators may include:

  • Sudden changes in behaviour, mood or presentation
  • Decline in attendance, engagement or academic performance
  • Signs of neglect or physical, emotional or sexual abuse
  • Disclosure of harm, fear or unsafe circumstances
  • Online harm, including cyberbullying, grooming or exploitation
  • Concerns about radicalisation or extremism

An indicator does not automatically prove abuse, but it may require further assessment or action.

5. Prevent Duty

The MentorSphere recognises the responsibility to identify, report and respond to concerns relating to radicalisation and extremism. Concerns should be recorded and reported to the Designated Safeguarding Lead without delay.

6. Reporting procedure

  1. Document the concern promptly, clearly and objectively. Record what was seen, heard or disclosed, using the person's own words where possible.
  2. Contact the Designated Safeguarding Lead, Luke Turner, without delay.
  3. The Designated Safeguarding Lead will assess the information and decide whether advice, referral or emergency escalation is required.
  4. Information will be shared only with those who need it for safeguarding or legal purposes.

Promises of complete confidentiality must not be made where a safeguarding concern exists.

7. Online safety and session access

  • Live lessons are delivered through approved, password-protected platforms such as Lessonspace.
  • Guest access is disabled where the platform allows.
  • Learners use individual access arrangements or links that are not shared publicly.
  • A waiting room or identity check is used where available, and only expected participants are admitted.
  • Screen sharing, file sharing and chat remain under tutor control.
  • Unknown or inappropriate users are removed and the incident is recorded.
  • Personal information must remain confidential during online sessions.
  • Inappropriate online behaviour or contact should be reported immediately.

8. Session recording

  • Sessions are recorded only with explicit prior consent.
  • Recordings are used only for safeguarding, quality assurance, training or agreed educational support.
  • Recordings are not shared externally unless required by law or safeguarding duties.
  • Recordings are stored securely in line with the Privacy Policy.
  • Consent can be withdrawn by emailing luke@thementorsphere.co.uk.
  • If a safeguarding concern arises during a recorded session, the relevant material may be reviewed and escalated appropriately.

9. Data protection and retention

Safeguarding information is stored securely and handled in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Safeguarding records may be retained for six years or until the learner reaches age 25, whichever is longer, where this is required by the policy and relevant obligations.

10. Mental health and wellbeing

  • Learners' mental health and emotional wellbeing are treated seriously.
  • Concerns are responded to sensitively and within the limits of the service.
  • Reasonable session adaptations and signposting may be offered.
  • The MentorSphere is not a crisis or clinical mental health service.

11. Training and awareness

The Designated Safeguarding Lead undertakes safeguarding training and updates knowledge regularly. Relevant temporary or cover staff must understand and follow this policy before working with learners.

12. Complaints and whistleblowing

Safeguarding complaints should be directed to Luke Turner. Where a concern is unresolved, serious or relates to the Designated Safeguarding Lead, advice may be sought from:

  • The NSPCC Helpline: 0808 800 5000
  • The relevant local authority safeguarding service
  • The NSPCC Whistleblowing Advice Line: 0800 028 0285
  • Emergency services where there is immediate danger

13. Communication and review

This policy is made available to learners, families and staff. It is reviewed at least annually, and earlier where legislation, guidance, platforms or service delivery change.

By engaging with The MentorSphere, learners and families acknowledge that safeguarding information has been made available. Safeguarding action does not depend on consent where there is a lawful need to protect someone from harm.
