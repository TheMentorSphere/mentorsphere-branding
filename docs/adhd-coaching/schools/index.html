Education settings

Working alongside schools

With appropriate consent, ADHD coaching can include proportionate communication with a school or education provider to support a more joined-up approach.

Discuss school involvement Read about consent and privacy

School involvement is optional. It is only considered when it may benefit the young person.

When involvement may help

Connect coaching strategies with everyday education

School or education-provider involvement may be useful when a young person would benefit from more consistent understanding across coaching, home and education.

The aim is a proportionate, joined-up approach. It does not make the school part of every coaching session, and it is not required for coaching to continue.

Possible areas of focus

  • Engagement and participation
  • Emotional regulation
  • Executive functioning
  • Routines, transitions and organisation
  • Communication around agreed support strategies

Consent and confidentiality

The young person remains central

Any school communication is agreed in advance and respects the young person's age-appropriate privacy.

Appropriate consent

School involvement only takes place where it may benefit the young person and appropriate consent has been obtained.

Agreed information

The family and young person agree what can be shared. Private session content is not automatically passed to education staff.

Family involvement

Parent or guardian involvement is agreed according to the young person's age, needs, preferences and circumstances. Safeguarding responsibilities continue throughout.

Agreed, proportionate involvement may include

  • Communication with relevant staff
  • Sharing agreed strategies
  • Contributing to a joined-up support approach
  • Helping staff understand support for engagement, emotional regulation and executive functioning

Working together

Clear communication with relevant staff

Where agreed, communication can help education staff understand the practical strategies being explored through coaching.

The level of involvement is kept focused on the young person's goals. It does not give a school automatic access to coaching records or every detail discussed in a session.

Charges and quotations

Additional liaison is agreed before work begins

School consultations, reports, liaison and separately commissioned support are not automatically included within the standard coaching session price.

The proposed scope, people involved and any additional costs are agreed in advance. A bespoke quotation is provided where additional work is requested.

School involvement

Bespoke quotation

Charges depend on the agreed consultation, liaison, reporting or commissioned work. No additional work begins without the scope and cost being agreed.

Common questions

Before involving a school

Is school involvement required?

No. It is an optional part of coaching and is only considered when it may benefit the young person and appropriate consent can be obtained.

Who decides what is shared?

The information and purpose are agreed with the family and young person in advance. The young person's voice, privacy and growing independence remain central.

Is school liaison included in the coaching price?

Not automatically. Any consultation, report, liaison or separately commissioned support is scoped and quoted before the additional work begins.

Discuss a joined-up approach

Explain what school involvement might help with. The appropriate consent, scope and any additional quotation can then be discussed.
