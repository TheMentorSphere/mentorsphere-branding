ADHD coaching through Access to Work

Access to Work may fund practical support, including ADHD coaching, when a disability or health condition affects someone at work.

What Access to Work is

Support for staying in or moving into paid work

Access to Work is a UK government scheme that may help fund practical support when ADHD, another disability or a health condition affects someone at work.

It may be relevant to employees, self-employed people and some people who are about to begin or return to paid work. Eligibility depends on personal and work circumstances.

A formal ADHD diagnosis is not always required to apply. Access to Work may ask for information about how a condition or difficulty affects the person at work.

Important funding limits

  • Access to Work makes every eligibility and funding decision
  • Funding is not guaranteed
  • The amount and type of support approved may vary
  • Written approval should be received before funding is relied upon

How to apply

  1. Check your eligibility through the official GOV.UK Access to Work service.
  2. Apply online or contact the Access to Work helpline.
  3. Explain how ADHD affects you within your work and what support you believe would help.
  4. Request specialist ADHD coaching as part of the support being considered.
  5. Contact The MentorSphere for a written quotation and description of the proposed coaching.
  6. Wait for written confirmation of the approved support before relying on Access to Work to cover the coaching cost.

Application steps

Describe the impact at work

The application should explain the practical effect of ADHD or another condition within the person's work. It can also describe why specialist ADHD coaching may help.

The client remains responsible for understanding the terms of an award and making sure funding has been approved.

Common questions

Access to Work funding

Is an ADHD diagnosis required?

Not always. Access to Work may instead ask for evidence or information about how a condition or difficulty affects you at work. Access to Work decides what information is required.

Can The MentorSphere guarantee funding?

No. The MentorSphere can provide a quotation and service outline, but Access to Work makes all funding decisions and may approve a different level or type of support.

Should coaching start before written approval?

Funding should not be assumed until written confirmation has been received. If someone chooses to begin earlier, responsibility for payment must be agreed separately.

Can support continue when an award ends?

A renewal or extension may be considered by Access to Work, but it is not guaranteed. Coaching may pause when the approved funding period ends or while further approval is outstanding.
