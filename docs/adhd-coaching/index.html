Understand your brain. Harness your strengths.

ADHD coaching built around how you actually work

Online ADHD coaching supports self-understanding, executive functioning and practical change. Sessions are calm, collaborative and shaped around the person's strengths, needs, communication preferences and current capacity.

A session starts with a low-pressure check-in, agrees a useful focus, explores what is underneath the pattern and finishes with one clear, realistic next step.

The approach is personalised, mentoring-led and lived-experience-informed. It is grounded in more than ten years working across education and SEND settings, without assuming that one strategy will work for everyone.

Who ADHD coaching is for

Personalised support at different stages of life

Age 18+

Adults with ADHD

Practical and reflective support for understanding how ADHD affects daily life. Coaching can help with planning, motivation, productivity, emotional regulation, relationships, confidence and building sustainable routines or ways of working.

Ages 10 to 17

Young people with ADHD

Personalised coaching shaped around the young person's strengths, needs, interests and goals. Sessions can explore confidence, emotional regulation, school pressures, routines, growing independence and understanding ADHD without shame. Appropriate safeguarding and consent arrangements are agreed before coaching begins.

Family support

Parents & guardians supporting a young person with ADHD

Practical support to understand a young person's ADHD and respond more effectively. Coaching can explore supportive routines, co-regulation, reducing conflict or overwhelm, compassionate boundaries and growing independence.

Supporting young people works best together

Parent or guardian involvement can help strategies carry over into everyday life. The exact balance is agreed individually, but I will often recommend that around one in every four sessions involves a parent or guardian.

This is a general recommendation rather than a fixed requirement. The young person's voice, privacy and growing independence remain central.

International ADHD coaching

Online ADHD coaching for international clients

Sessions are delivered online, so location does not have to be a barrier. Enquiries are welcome from the United States and other countries where a suitable session time and appropriate coaching arrangement can be agreed.

The MentorSphere remains a UK-based service. Availability for international clients depends on time-zone compatibility, and the scope of coaching stays the same wherever a client lives.

International coaching focuses on transferable areas such as executive functioning, routines, organisation, task initiation, motivation, emotional regulation, study, work and everyday life. The MentorSphere's education and SEND support remains UK-focused.

What stays consistent

What coaching can support

What ADHD and executive-function coaching can help with

There is no fixed curriculum. Coaching explores what is actually happening underneath a difficulty, then builds strategies that fit the person's life rather than relying on perfect motivation.

Context matters. Where relevant, sessions can also explore LGBTQIA+ identity, masking, recovery from addiction, disrupted routines, illness, shame and the gap between ability and output.

Lived insight supports empathy and practical understanding, but does not replace professional boundaries or appropriate clinical care.

  • Emotional regulation and recovering after difficult moments
  • Routines, transitions, organisation and remembering everyday tasks
  • Planning, prioritising, time management and breaking work into usable steps
  • Motivation, procrastination and task initiation
  • School, university or workplace strategies
  • Relationships, communication and rejection sensitivity
  • Identity, masking, confidence and self-advocacy
  • Preparing for appointments, reviews or support conversations

ADHD coaching, therapy and clinical treatment

ADHD coaching is not therapy, psychotherapy, medical treatment, crisis support or a clinical diagnostic assessment. The MentorSphere cannot diagnose ADHD. Coaching can sit alongside healthcare, counselling or education support, but it does not replace them. Where a need falls outside coaching, this will be discussed honestly and appropriate signposting may be suggested.

Common questions

Online ADHD coaching questions

Can I access The MentorSphere's ADHD coaching from the United States?

Yes, suitable enquiries from the United States and other countries are welcome. The MentorSphere is based in the UK, and international sessions depend on compatible time zones and availability. The coaching scope and professional boundaries stay the same wherever a client lives.

Is ADHD coaching available online?

Yes. Sessions are delivered online using an approved platform such as Lessonspace or Google Meet. A workable platform and any accessibility adjustments can be discussed before coaching begins.

What can ADHD coaching help with?

Coaching can explore executive functioning, planning, organisation, time management, routines, task initiation, procrastination, motivation, emotional regulation, communication and practical strategies for study, work and everyday life. The focus is agreed around the person's priorities.

Is ADHD coaching the same as therapy?

No. ADHD coaching is practical, reflective and strategy-based. It is not therapy, clinical diagnosis, medical treatment or crisis support, although it may sit alongside appropriate healthcare or counselling.

Do I need a formal ADHD diagnosis?

No formal diagnosis is required to enquire. If you are diagnosed, awaiting assessment or exploring whether ADHD may be relevant, suitability can be discussed during the free discovery call. Coaching focuses on the needs and patterns you describe and cannot provide a diagnosis.

Can parents or young people access ADHD coaching?

Coaching is available to young people aged 10 to 17 and to parents or guardians supporting a young person with ADHD. Safeguarding, consent, privacy and the appropriate level of family involvement are agreed before coaching begins.
