For employers and organisations

Employer-funded ADHD coaching

Confidential one-to-one online coaching that can support sustainable working, communication, confidence and executive functioning.

Discuss workplace support Read about confidentiality

Funding coaching does not give an employer automatic access to session content or private coaching records.

Who the service is for

Individual coaching commissioned by a workplace

Employers and organisations can fund ADHD coaching for a member of their team. Coaching is tailored to the employee rather than delivered through a fixed workplace programme.

The employee and coach agree the practical focus. Employer, manager or HR involvement is limited to what has been explicitly agreed.

Employer-funded coaching costs £110 per 60-minute session.

Typical coaching areas

  • Planning, prioritisation and workload management
  • Organisation, focus and managing distractions
  • Communication and working relationships
  • Confidence and self-understanding
  • Emotional regulation
  • Sustainable routines and ways of working

Confidentiality and consent

Clear boundaries before coaching begins

The coaching relationship remains confidential. Any reporting or liaison is agreed in advance with the employee.

Explicit consent

Information is only shared with an employer, manager or HR representative when the employee has given explicit consent and the information has been agreed in advance.

Agreed reporting

Any progress summary, workplace discussion or final review is limited to the agreed purpose and boundaries recorded before the work begins.

Private session content

Employers do not receive automatic access to coaching conversations or private records. Confidentiality remains subject to safeguarding and legal responsibilities.

Where agreed, support may include

  • An initial discussion about coaching goals
  • Agreed progress summaries
  • Liaison with a manager or HR representative
  • Workplace strategy discussions
  • Discussion of possible reasonable adjustments, without providing legal advice
  • A final review of progress and next steps

Joined-up workplace support

Involvement is proportionate and agreed

Some arrangements benefit from a limited conversation with a manager or HR representative. This can help connect coaching strategies with the employee's working environment.

The scope, goals, reporting expectations and people involved are recorded before work begins. The employee remains central to decisions about what is shared.

Additional workplace support

Consultations and workshops by arrangement

Limited workplace consultations and bespoke ADHD workshops may be available where they fit the organisation's needs and The MentorSphere's current service scope.

These services are separately scoped and provided through a bespoke quotation. Full training days are not currently part of the standard service.

Employer-funded coaching

£110 per 60-minute session

Workplace consultations and ADHD workshops are quoted separately. Self-funded coaching package discounts do not automatically apply.

Common questions

Before a workplace arrangement begins

Will the employer receive session notes?

No automatic access is provided. Any summary or report must have an agreed purpose, explicit employee consent and clear boundaries about what will be shared.

Can a manager help set coaching goals?

An initial goal discussion can be included where the employee agrees. The individual coaching relationship and the employee's own goals remain central.

Can coaching discuss reasonable adjustments?

Possible adjustments and helpful workplace strategies can be discussed. The MentorSphere does not provide legal advice or make decisions for the employer.
