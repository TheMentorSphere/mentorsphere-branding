# The MentorSphere Website Positioning Refinement Brief

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 26 July 2026  
**Use with:** `AGENTS.md`

## Decision statement

The website should not emphasise coaching and support more than tutoring, but it should stop presenting tutoring as the implied centre of the business. The desired result is a neutral, joined-up message in which tutoring, ADHD coaching and education/SEND support are three equal pathways within one service.

This is a positioning refinement, not a visual rebuild. Preserve the existing brand, motion layer, accessibility approach and overall quality.

## 1. Why the current site feels tutoring-heavy

The current draft already includes strong joined-up language, but several prominent cues still frame the whole business through tutoring:

- Three subject branch logos are visually prominent beside two non-tutoring branch logos.
- The trust strip says `10+ years: Teaching experience across varied settings`.
- The About hero says `More than tutoring`.
- The founder heading says `Teaching experience shaped by very different classrooms`.
- Some examples assume a person starts with maths and later accesses coaching or SEND support.
- `Explore the services` currently links to tutoring rather than a neutral overview.
- The onboarding sequence presents a subject baseline as a central step for everyone.

Do not solve this by making coaching or support dominant. The goal is balance.

## 2. Target positioning

A visitor should understand within the first screen and the first 15 seconds:

- The MentorSphere is one joined-up education and neurodiversity support service.
- It offers three equal pathways: learning and tutoring; ADHD coaching; education and SEND support.
- A person can start with whichever pathway matches their current need.
- Support can evolve without repeated onboarding and disconnected providers.
- The service is personalised, mentoring-led, neurodiversity-affirming and online across the UK.

**Recommended umbrella line:** Joined-up support for learning, neurodiversity and education.

## 3. Messaging rules

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Lead with the umbrella service before individual offers | Lead with tutoring and add coaching and support as extras |
| Use education and SEND experience for Luke's background | Use teaching experience as the main or only proof point |
| Show all three pathways as equal starting points | Assume a person starts with maths |
| Use neutral onboarding that adapts to the service | Apply subject baselines and learner terminology to everyone |
| Keep strong standalone service pages | Make the offer so vague that visitors cannot understand it |
| Keep tutoring prominent and credible | Reduce tutoring so much that current families think it is being abandoned |

## 4. Site-wide wording changes

| Current | Recommended |
|---|---|
| `10+ years: Teaching experience across varied settings` | `10+ years: Across education and SEND settings` |
| `More than tutoring, without losing the personal relationship` | `Joined-up support across learning, neurodiversity and education` |
| `Teaching experience shaped by very different classrooms` | `Experience shaped across education, SEND and learner support` |
| `I have more than ten years of teaching experience...` | `I have more than ten years' experience working across education and SEND...` |
| `A learner may first arrive for maths...` | `A person may begin with tutoring, ADHD coaching or education/SEND support, then move between services as needs change.` |
| Footer led by a list of services | `Joined-up education and neurodiversity support, including personalised tutoring, ADHD coaching and education/SEND guidance.` |
| `Explore the services` links to tutoring | Link to a neutral service overview or the three-pathway section |

Recommended homepage metadata:

- **Title:** The MentorSphere | Joined-up Education and Neurodiversity Support
- **Description:** Personalised tutoring, ADHD coaching and education/SEND support for children, young people, adults and families across the UK, delivered through one joined-up, mentoring-led service.
- **Open Graph:** Joined-up, neurodiversity-affirming support across learning, ADHD coaching and education/SEND.
- **Structured data:** A UK online education and neurodiversity support service providing personalised tutoring, ADHD coaching and education/SEND support.

## 5. Homepage

### Hero

- **Eyebrow:** Joined-up, neurodiversity-affirming support across the UK
- **H1:** Joined-up support for learning, neurodiversity and education
- **Lead:** The MentorSphere brings personalised tutoring, ADHD coaching and education/SEND support together in one consistent, human-first service for children, young people, adults and families.
- **Primary CTA:** Book a free introduction
- **Secondary CTA:** Explore the services; link to the three-pathway overview, not directly to tutoring.

### Trust strip

| Trust point | Wording |
|---|---|
| 10+ years | Experience across education and SEND settings |
| Fully personalised | Plans shaped around strengths, needs, goals and circumstances |
| One joined-up service | Tutoring, ADHD coaching and education/SEND support |
| Online across the UK | Flexible support without travel or unfamiliar spaces |

### Three equal pathway cards

1. **Learning and tutoring:** Personalised one-to-one support in maths, English and science, shaped around confidence, goals and the learner's preferred ways of thinking.
2. **ADHD coaching:** Practical and reflective support for executive function, routines, motivation, emotions, identity and everyday life.
3. **Education and SEND support:** Clear help to organise evidence, understand options, prepare communications and navigate education systems.

Give the cards equal width, visual weight, heading level and CTA treatment. The tutoring card may reference the three subject branches without using three times as much imagery.

### Joined-up model

Recommended copy:

> Someone may first come to The MentorSphere for tutoring, ADHD coaching or help navigating education and SEND. Their priorities may change over time. The service is designed so the understanding built at the beginning can travel with them, reducing repeated explanations and disconnected advice.

### Getting started

1. Share the background: goals, strengths, circumstances and support needs.
2. Book a free introduction: meet online, ask questions and explore the right starting point.
3. Agree the starting point: a gentle academic baseline for tutoring, or a needs and priorities review for coaching or support.
4. Receive a clear plan: strengths, barriers, adaptations and suggested next steps.
5. Begin at the right pace: choose the service, schedule and support level that fit.

## 6. About page

- **Hero H1:** Joined-up support across learning, neurodiversity and education
- **Lead:** The MentorSphere was created to bring tutoring, coaching and education/SEND support into one consistent service for people whose needs do not fit neatly within one subject, diagnosis or stage of life.
- **Founder heading:** Experience shaped across education, SEND and learner support
- **Proof point:** More than ten years working across education and SEND settings

Recommended founder copy:

> I have more than ten years' experience working across education and SEND, including mainstream schools, specialist provisions, prison education and one-to-one support. These settings showed me that effective support cannot be reduced to one teaching method, one subject or one label. I created The MentorSphere to bring learning, coaching and education support together in a flexible model that considers confidence, executive function, communication and the wider realities surrounding education.

Recommended lived-experience copy:

> I have ADHD, and lived experience informs the way I understand inconsistency, overwhelm, motivation and the importance of feeling genuinely understood. That insight supports the work alongside clear boundaries, safeguarding and ongoing professional development.

Recommended feature list:

- More than ten years across education and SEND.
- Mainstream, specialist, alternative and prison education settings.
- Children, young people and adult clients.
- SEND, SEMH and neurodivergent support.
- Academic learning, coaching and education planning.
- ADHD lived experience and recovery-informed practice.
- Online one-to-one mentoring and support.

## 7. Service, pricing and contact pages

### Tutoring

- Keep detailed, confident and clearly academic.
- Identify tutoring as one of three main pathways.
- Do not dilute subject coverage, exam support or one-to-one learning.
- Add a subtle cross-link to coaching and SEND support where useful.

### ADHD coaching

- Present as an established, equal pathway.
- Retain strategy-based, neurodiversity-affirming and lived-experience-informed wording.
- Keep the non-therapy and non-diagnostic boundary.
- Address young people, adults and parents where current policy permits.

### Education and SEND support

- Use `Education and SEND Support` publicly where clearer; retain `The Support Mentor` as the branch identity.
- Explain practical outputs such as organising evidence, preparing communications and understanding options.
- Avoid legal-advice and guaranteed-outcome wording.
- Make clear that a client can start here without tutoring or coaching.

### Pricing

- Open with three equal service categories rather than tutoring as the assumed default.
- Do not change any figure, discount, package or policy without a confirmed source.
- Explain different pricing models rather than forcing one table.
- Include a neutral route for mixed or uncertain needs.

### Contact

- Offer equal enquiry options: tutoring; ADHD coaching; education/SEND support; unsure or mixed needs.
- Use `person` or `client` in general instructions rather than `learner` only.
- Describe the introduction as a needs and fit conversation; use a tutoring baseline only where relevant.
- Preserve safeguarding and privacy information.

## 8. Visual hierarchy

- Keep the main logo as the primary umbrella mark.
- Use three equal pathway cards as the main service navigation.
- Show the five branch logos as a secondary system if showing all five equally makes tutoring look dominant.
- Consider grouping the maths, English and science logos inside one tutoring pathway panel or orbit segment.
- Give the ADHD and Support pathway visuals equal scale and prominence.
- Preserve the motion and mosaic layer; do not add unnecessary movement.
- Keep policy pages visually quiet.

Preferred navigation scope: keep the existing three direct service links and use equal styling. Rename Support Services to Education & SEND Support only if it fits comfortably. Avoid a navigation rebuild unless needed.

## 9. Implementation boundaries

- Create a new branch such as `agent/website-positioning-refinement`.
- Read the project guide before editing.
- Preserve the current design, responsive behaviour, motion layer and accessibility.
- Do not alter prices, policy terms, contact details, booking links, safeguarding wording or service eligibility.
- Do not advertise future branches, accreditation, diagnostic services or legal representation.
- Do not merge, deploy or modify Pages or domain settings without approval.
- Update metadata and structured data where the old positioning appears.

Validation:

- Search all HTML files for teaching experience, more than tutoring, tutoring-first examples and the old footer description.
- Check titles, descriptions, Open Graph and JSON-LD.
- Run link and asset-path checks.
- Validate JavaScript if edited.
- Review homepage, About, all three service pages, pricing and contact at desktop and mobile widths.
- Confirm keyboard navigation, focus, reduced motion and contrast remain intact.

## 10. Acceptance criteria

- The first screen identifies one joined-up education and neurodiversity support service.
- All three pathways have equal conceptual and visual weight.
- Coaching and support are not framed as extras attached to tutoring.
- Coaching and support do not appear more important than tutoring.
- Luke's background is described as more than ten years across education and SEND.
- The introductory journey works for tutoring, coaching and support clients.
- Service pages remain specific and useful.
- SEO terms remain present without controlling the brand hierarchy.
- Metadata, structured data and footer copy are consistent.
- No prices, policies or operational commitments change.
- Accessibility and responsive behaviour pass existing checks.
