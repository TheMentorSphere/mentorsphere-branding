## Core positioning

The MentorSphere is a **joined-up education and neurodiversity support service**. It brings personalised tutoring, ADHD coaching and education/SEND support together through one consistent, mentoring-led relationship. Tutoring remains a core branch, but it must not be presented as the default service with coaching and support treated as add-ons.

## 1. How to use this guide

- Read this guide and the current task brief before inspecting or editing the repository.
- Priority order: Luke's latest explicit instruction; the current task brief; this guide; the latest approved website content; the latest relevant policy or pricing document; older planning material.

- If sources conflict, identify the conflict and ask. Do not silently choose one.
- Use a feature branch, focused commits and a reviewable pull request.

## 4. Current service scope

### Learning and tutoring

- Personalised one-to-one online tuition in maths, English and science.
- May include curriculum learning, Functional Skills, 11+, exam preparation, confidence, study skills and agreed bespoke learning plans.
- Sessions are shaped around strengths, interests, goals, communication preferences and current capacity.
- The personalised approach is open to a wide range of learners, not only neurodivergent learners.

### ADHD coaching

- Strategy-based coaching for young people, adults and parents, subject to the current policy and age criteria.
- Typical areas include routines, planning, organisation, motivation, productivity, emotional regulation, relationships and self-understanding.
- Lived-experience-informed and neurodiversity-affirming.
- Not therapy, diagnosis or crisis support.
- Access to Work support may be available where confirmed by current service information.

### Education and SEND support

- Help to understand education processes, organise information and prepare clear communications.
- May include EHCP-related support, education planning, home education or EOTAS context, exam access or private exam coordination, and SEND navigation where currently offered.
- Can include letters, evidence summaries, questions and action plans.
- Not legal representation, a clinical service or a statutory decision-maker.

Do not turn a possible, occasional or future activity into a permanent advertised service.

## 5. Audiences

- Children and young people from KS1 to KS4, including 11+, Functional Skills, home-educated and EOTAS learners.
- Adults returning to learning or seeking ADHD coaching.
- Parents and families seeking strategies, plans or education/SEND guidance.
- People with diagnosed or suspected ADHD and other neurodivergent profiles.
- People affected by disrupted education, anxiety, low confidence, gaps in knowledge, exam pressure or a poor fit with conventional teaching.

Use `learner` for tutoring, `client` for adult coaching or support where appropriate, and `person`, `individual`, `parent` or `family` for umbrella-level copy.

## 8. Voice and terminology

- Use UK English.
- Tone: professional, warm, human, calm and direct.
- Do not use em dashes or en dashes. Use colons, semicolons, commas or full stops.
- Lead with the person and the goal, not a diagnosis or deficit.
- Use plain language, short paragraphs, meaningful headings and concrete examples.
- Use `neurodivergent` for people and `neurodiversity` for the wider concept.
- Avoid `cure`, `fix`, `normalise`, `high functioning` and `low functioning`.
- Do not imply that diagnosis is required unless a specific service policy says so.
- Do not promise grades, diagnoses, EHCP outcomes, funding awards or guaranteed behavioural change.
- Use umbrella wording before listing individual services.

Preferred phrases:

- Joined-up education and neurodiversity support.
- Learning, coaching and education support.
- Works with the person, not a standard template.
- Education and SEND settings.
- Support can evolve as needs change.

## 10. Accessibility and user experience

- Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Preserve semantic headings, landmarks, labels and keyboard navigation.
- Provide visible focus states and adequate target sizes.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and data-saving settings.
- Do not communicate essential information through animation, hover, colour or position alone.
- Maintain strong contrast, comfortable line length and sufficient spacing.
- Avoid autoplay, rapid movement, parallax-heavy effects and persistent distraction.
- Keep content usable without JavaScript where practical.
- Use meaningful alt text for informative images and empty alt text for decorative images.
- Test desktop and mobile; do not assume hover exists.

## 12. Safeguarding, privacy and boundaries

The MentorSphere is **not** an emergency service, clinical diagnostic service, therapy provider or legal representation service.

- Safeguarding applies across work with children, young people and vulnerable clients.
- Luke Turner is the Designated Safeguarding Lead in the current policy.
- Confidentiality has safeguarding and serious-harm exceptions.
- Do not publish identifiable client information without explicit permission and a lawful privacy basis.
- ADHD coaching is proactive and strategy-based; it is not therapy or diagnosis.
- Education and SEND support can help with preparation, understanding and communication; it does not guarantee statutory outcomes or replace legal advice.
- Do not weaken privacy, consent, recording or safeguarding wording when editing for brevity.
